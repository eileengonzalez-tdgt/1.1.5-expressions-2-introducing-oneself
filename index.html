01 · Encounter: First Impressions

Read two introductions. Compare the audience, purpose, and tone.

☕ Conversation A · Social

“Hey! I'm Alex. I'm from Guatemala, and I'm really into music and traveling. What about you?”

💼 Conversation B · Professional

“Good morning! My name is Alex. I have experience working with customers, and I enjoy helping people solve problems. I'm excited to learn more about this opportunity.”

Discuss together

  1. Where might each introduction happen?
  2. How does the tone change?
  3. What personal details does Alex share?
  4. Which introduction invites the listener to respond?
Notice: Professional introductions focus on relevant background and purpose. Casual introductions often include personal interests and an invitation to connect.

02 · Clarify: Build a Better Introduction

Learn useful expressions, then combine them into a natural introduction.

Expression bank · Interactive cards

Click a card to reveal an example. Click again to return to the expression.

Four-part introduction builder

1 · Opening

Greet the person and say your name.

2 · Background

Share where you're from or what you do.

3 · Detail

Add an interest, skill, or relevant experience.

4 · Connection

Ask a question or explain your interest in the situation.

Casual example

“Hi, I'm Maya. I'm from Antigua, and I'm really into hiking. How about you?”

Professional example

“Good morning. My name is Maya. I have experience in customer support, and one of my strengths is staying calm under pressure. I'm excited about this opportunity.”

Say it aloud: Choose four expressions and build an introduction that sounds like you.

03 · Remember: Choose, Complete & Improve

10 self-checking questions. Select your answers, read the explanations, and try again.

Speaking bridge: Use two expressions from the quiz to create a short introduction. Add one question to invite a response.

04 · Internalize: Two Situations, Two Introductions

Work in pairs. Practice both situations and switch roles after each round.

☕ Round 1 · Meeting a New Friend (5 min)

Meet someone at a social event. Share your name, two personal details, and ask two follow-up questions.

Reveal guiding questions
  1. Where are you from?
  2. What do you enjoy doing outside work or class?
  3. How did you get interested in that?
  4. What about you?

💼 Round 2 · Meeting a Recruiter (5 min)

Introduce yourself for a customer service opportunity. Share your background, a useful skill, and your motivation.

Reveal guiding questions
  1. What experience or transferable skills do you have?
  2. What is one of your strengths?
  3. Why does this opportunity interest you?
  4. Can you give an example?

Partner feedback

Did the introduction match the audience? Was there a useful detail? Did the speaker respond or invite a follow-up?

05 · Fluent Use: Tell Me About Yourself

Give a 45–60 second response, then answer one or two questions from your partner.

🎤 “Could you tell me a little about yourself?”

Include your background, relevant experience or transferable skills, one personal strength, and why you're interested in the opportunity.

No work experience yet? Talk about school projects, volunteering, personal skills, or learning experiences.

Partner follow-up questions

  1. What do you enjoy most about working with people?
  2. Can you tell me more about that experience?
  3. What is a skill you're currently developing?
Success criteria: Speak clearly for 45–60 seconds, organize your introduction logically, use relevant details, and respond naturally to follow-up questions.
